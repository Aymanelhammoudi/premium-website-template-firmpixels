Ambition and future

Keep learning.Keep building.

Where I want to go, what I want to develop and the type of working environment in which I can contribute.

What drives me

Growing throughpractical experience.

I want to develop into a versatile commercial professional who understands customers, takes responsibility and can turn ideas into action.

I grow by doing: holding conversations, asking for feedback, building product knowledge, working with experienced colleagues and reviewing what could be improved.

I am looking for an environment where performance, development and reliability reinforce one another.

My direction

Three goals forthe coming years.

For me, ambition means continuing to learn and improving the quality of my work step by step.

  1. 01

    Deepen my commercial expertise.

    Develop further in needs analysis, advice, product knowledge, relationship building and bringing sales conversations to a professional close.

  2. 02

    Take broader responsibility.

    Learn more about planning, teamwork, customer retention and guiding a process from first contact to result.

  3. 03

    Build a strong business.

    Professionalise my experience in web design and services through better processes, stronger projects and lasting client relationships.
